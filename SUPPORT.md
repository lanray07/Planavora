# Planavora support

To report an app issue, open a new issue in the [Planavora issue tracker](https://github.com/lanray07/Planavora/issues). Include the app version, tablet model, Fire OS or Android version and the steps that led to the issue. Use fictional names and amounts in screenshots.

## Common questions

- Where is my event data? Event records and copied attachments are stored on your device.
- Why is the microphone unavailable? Voice recognition depends on the speech service installed on the device. Open Voice commands to use typed input.
- How do I restore Premium? Open More → Planavora Premium → Restore purchases while connected to Amazon and signed in with the account that purchased it.
- How can I remove event records? Delete an individual event from the Dashboard or choose Delete all event data in Settings.
