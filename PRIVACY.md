# Planavora privacy policy

Effective November 13, 2026. Planavora is provided by Lanray. You can contact the developer about privacy or deletion using the [Planavora support page](https://github.com/lanray07/Planavora/blob/main/SUPPORT.md).

Planavora stores event plans, guest records, budgets, preferences and copies of attachments in its private storage on your device. It uses an Android system picker when you choose a file to attach or a destination for a report. The app does not require a Planavora account or include an advertising or analytics SDK.

Voice recording starts when you use the voice control and grant the microphone permission. Your installed recognition service processes the audio and may use its own internet service. Planavora keeps a transcription in the voice screen for your review and does not save audio recordings. You can use typed commands without microphone access. You can turn off spoken responses in Settings.

Amazon processes Premium purchases and handles payment details under its own privacy notice. When Planavora verifies a purchase or restore, it sends the Amazon user ID and receipt ID over HTTPS to the Planavora verifier hosted on Cloudflare Workers. The verifier passes those identifiers to Amazon’s Receipt Verification Service to check that the receipt belongs to Planavora Premium and is still valid. The verifier processes the identifiers for each request and does not store them in a server database or write them to application logs. Cloudflare and Amazon may process connection and service information under their own privacy terms. Planavora stores the resulting Premium status and verified receipt ID on your device. Purchase verification does not include event or guest records.

Use Settings → Delete all event data to remove event data and attachments. Shared and exported copies are managed by their recipients and by the storage locations you chose. Amazon manages purchase records in your Amazon account. To ask about access, correction, deletion or other privacy rights that apply to you, contact the developer through the support page. You can also raise a concern with your local data protection authority where applicable.

For support, use the [Planavora GitHub support information](https://github.com/lanray07/Planavora/blob/main/SUPPORT.md). Please keep guest information, financial records and receipts out of public issue reports.
